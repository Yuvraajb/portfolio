I've rebuilt my personal site more times than I've shipped most of my side projects, which is either a great joke about developers or a fairly accurate description of my last five years. This time the goal was different: build something small enough that I'd actually keep it running.

So here's the plan. Long posts like this one live under [Blog](/blog/) and the latest few show up on the homepage. There's a synced [Projects](/projects/) feed for what I'm actually building, and a [Bookshelf](/bookshelf/) for what I'm reading.

No CMS, no database, no build step that takes longer than my coffee. Just markdown files and a small script that turns them into HTML. More on that soon.
